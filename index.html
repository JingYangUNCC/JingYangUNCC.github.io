Jing Yang

Professor, Department of Machine Learning, University of North Carolina at Charlotte

My research helps people make sense of complex data. Today I focus on human–AI collaborative analytics: how people and AI can analyze data together. This builds on two decades of work on visual analytics techniques for many kinds of data, including multidimensional, time-oriented, network and hierarchical, text, trajectory, image and video data, and on core visualization questions such as interaction, insight management, clustering-based approaches, animation and LLM-assisted visualization. With my collaborators and students, I have also conducted design studies in sports, bioinformatics, finance, network security, health and education.
